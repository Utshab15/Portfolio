The person behind the keyboard

I'm Utshab Adhikari — an 18-year-old developer from Nepal with a genuine passion for learning, building, and breaking things (then fixing them).

I started my coding journey after completing my Grade 10 (SEE) exams in April 2024. During that vacation I dove headfirst into Python and Discord bot development. I then joined Plus 2 Science with Computer as a major, where I picked up Photoshop and frontend web development in Grade 11.

In Grade 12 I levelled up significantly — I explored IoT with Arduino and built a fully functional weather-forecasting device using Arduino Cloud, and I also learned PHP for backend development. I recently completed my Grade 12 NEB board exams in May 2026 and I'm ready for whatever comes next.

Outside of code, I've moderated Discord servers, owned and developed Minecraft servers, and I'm always chasing something new to learn.
